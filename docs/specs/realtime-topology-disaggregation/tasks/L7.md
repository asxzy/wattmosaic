# L7 — Historical reconciliation and versioned replay

**Wave:** 5 · **Depends on:** I7, L1, S17 · **Size:** L · **Decisions:** DEC-35 · **Design:** §9 (reconciliation) · **Analysis:** `analysis/F-…` WP-F7 · **Closes:** AT-19

## Objective
Replay immutable evidence (event groups, evidence and aggregates) through the pure `infer_group`/`infer_steady` with an **explicit** (model set, topology version, mapping version), writing into a versioned derived namespace. Measurements and evidence are never written. HA statistics are not rewritten (DEC-35).

## Owns
- new `engine/wattmosaic_engine/learning/reconciliation.py`
- new `engine/wattmosaic_engine/storage/migrations/v020_derived_runs.py` (`derived_runs`, `derived_estimates`; L2). Reserve 20 in implementation-plan §5 when you start.
- `tools/audit/replay_capture.py` (accept `models=ModelSet`)
- `engine/wattmosaic_engine/aggregation/aggregator.py`, `aggregation/models.py`, `aggregation/repository.py` (add version tags to buckets and runs)
- `tests/simulation/test_replay_versions.py`, new `tests/unit/test_reconciliation_runs.py`
- `tests/acceptance/test_at19_replay_new_model.py` (remove xfail)

## Steps
1. `Reconciler.run(range, triple, reason) -> derived_run_id` (chunked jobs on the background executor). Triggers:
   - promotion of a model, topology or mapping version, for the affected subjects and time;
   - late evidence;
   - a manual run via the diagnostics route.
2. Previous derived runs are kept and marked superseded. `GET /provenance/derived_run/{id}` is resolved through D4.
3. Extend the replay fingerprint gate with the model-set id.

## Tests
- **AT-19:** one simulated week replayed with model sets v1 and v2. Derived outputs differ as expected. `ct_samples` and evidence bytes are identical before and after. Both runs are retrievable.
- Replay after raw samples are pruned (evidence only) reproduces the live result within tolerance (HB §15).
- Replay never touches HA statistics paths (static test: no HA client import).
