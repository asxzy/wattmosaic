# P5 — Event group formation and late-arrival revision

**Wave:** 3 · **Depends on:** P4, P1 · **Size:** L · **Decisions:** DEC-15, 17 · **Design:** §4 (grouping, lifecycle, late data) · **Analysis:** `analysis/B-…` C5, C6, WP-B5

## Objective
Assemble `EventGroup`s from reader steps, HA events (linked and ring-held unlinked) and plug steps:
- each sub-event keeps its own timestamps;
- grouping uses context lineage, temporal overlap and topology co-movement;
- groups follow the HB §12.3 lifecycle;
- late data creates a new **revision** and leaves unrelated groups untouched.

## Owns
- new `engine/wattmosaic_engine/realtime/grouping.py`
- `engine/wattmosaic_engine/disaggregation/isolation.py`: mark `group_events` deprecated; adapt `reclassify` and `physical_device_events` to accept `EventGroup` (Z2 deletes the old path)
- new `tests/unit/test_grouping.py`

## Steps
1. Union-find per design §4. Co-movement uses `TopologyView` (K08 fake in tests; T1 in production) for CONFIRMED or PROVISIONAL CONTAINS.
2. Lifecycle and timers are driven by the injected clock. Emit `on_ready(group)` at MATCHING, and `on_revised(group)` when a revision is created after PROVISIONAL or CONFIRMED.
3. Persistence: write each revision through S16 `put_group_revision`. When a group first becomes evidence, persist the ring-held unlinked HA events that it references (DEC-17).
4. Replace `_collect_transitions` semantics: two transitions of one entity are two sub-events.

## Tests
- Two lights switched by one automation (same parent context, 2 s apart) → one group, two HA sub-events with distinct timestamps.
- A decoy shared context with physical changes 60 s apart is **not** grouped on context alone.
- One entity toggled twice in 5 s → two sub-events.
- **AT-18 support:** a group CONFIRMED, then a Circuit-A sample arriving 20 s late → revision 2 REVISED, revision 1 unchanged on disk, an unrelated concurrent group unchanged.
- A fridge step with no HA event becomes a group with only a reader sub-event.
- An HA-only change with no reader step stays DETECTED (no watts) until its window closes, then UNRESOLVED.
