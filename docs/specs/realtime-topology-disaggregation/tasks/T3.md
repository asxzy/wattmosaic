# T3 — Per-reader residual engine and learned unknown-load prior

**Wave:** 3 · **Depends on:** T2, S19, K08 · **Size:** M · **Decisions:** DEC-23, DEF-05 · **Design:** §6 · **Analysis:** `analysis/C-…` C5, C7, WP-C5

## Objective
Compute the signed residual R_r for **every** reader, mains and parents included, persisted per reader and topology version. Publish the `UnknownPrior` (μ_U, σ_U, λ_u) that I3 uses as the learned unknown-load penalty, and the over/under-assignment diagnostic.

## Owns
- new `engine/wattmosaic_engine/residual/__init__.py`, `residual/reader_residual.py`
- `engine/wattmosaic_engine/models/residual_power.py`: reuse its signed-residual and basis-compatibility code; delete the install-wide window path in Z2
- new `engine/wattmosaic_engine/api/routes/residuals_v3.py` (`GET /residuals?reader_id=`)
- new `tests/unit/test_reader_residual.py`

## Steps
1. `ReaderResidualEngine.update(result: JointInferenceResult, held: Mapping[str, HeldReading], operator)` → `ReaderResidualPoint`s:
   - R_r = Y_r − Σ β·Y_c − Σ H_ri·P̂_i;
   - identified subjects and group sums count once;
   - None if Y_r is None.

   Persist in batches (S19).
2. A background job (P6 executor) recomputes the `UnknownPrior` per scope every 15 min from the last 7 days of unexplained steps. It implements `ResidualModelView`.
3. Diagnostic per reader per day: |ΔY| share to ΔU vs subjects. Expose it through the route.
4. Noise σ_r comes from P2. **Do not** re-estimate noise here (rule 5: noise ≠ residual ≠ profile).

## Tests
- Two scopes with different loads get different baselines (regression for DEF-05: one install-wide window).
- The signed identity holds and is never clamped.
- A missing Y_r gives a None residual.
- λ_u decreases as the unexplained step rate rises.
- The initial λ_u favours residual: on an ambiguous synthetic case, more power lands in ΔU than in a weak candidate (with I3's fake solver).
- Noise, residual and profile are three distinct objects with distinct storage.
