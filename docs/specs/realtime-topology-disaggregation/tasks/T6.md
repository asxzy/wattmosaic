# T6 — Unknown Load Profile engine

**Wave:** 4 · **Depends on:** T3, P5, S19 · **Size:** M · **Decisions:** DEC-04 (promotion origin) · **Design:** §6 (profiles) · **Analysis:** `analysis/C-…` WP-C6 · **Closes:** AT-04

## Objective
Turn unexplained FINAL steps into persistent Unknown Load Profiles: per scope, located at the deepest reader that saw them, lifecycle never auto-CONFIRMED. Expose them as matching candidates (I1) and as matrix rows distinct from reader residual rows (M1).

## Owns
- new `engine/wattmosaic_engine/residual/unknown_profiles.py`
- new `engine/wattmosaic_engine/api/routes/unknown_profiles.py` (`GET /unknown-profiles`, `POST /unknown-profiles/{id}/promote` → creates a `PhysicalDevice` with `origin=unknown_profile_promotion` through D2's service)
- new `tests/unit/test_unknown_profiles.py`
- `tests/acceptance/test_at04_unknown_without_ha.py` (remove xfail)

## Steps
1. Consume `JointInferenceResult`s where the `unknown_scope` candidate explains ≥ 80 % of a reader step, or the group is UNRESOLVED.
2. Cluster per scope by (sign, |Δ| ± 20 %, duration bucket). Detect periodicity (the P3 tag).
3. Lifecycle: OBSERVING → CHARACTERISED (≥ 5 independent occurrences) → CANDIDATE (offered to the user). Retire after 30 days unseen; retired profiles are kept.
4. Provide `UnknownProfileView.profiles_for_scope(reader_id)` for I1's candidate source 6.

## Tests
- **AT-04:** an X07 fridge without HA → event groups → a profile CHARACTERISED after 5 cycles at the Circuit-B scope.
- A profile is never CONFIRMED.
- Promotion creates a device and a mapping edge (PROVISIONAL) and leaves the profile `promoted`.
- A residual row is never counted as a device: the physical total is unchanged by profile creation.
