# D2 — Endpoint discovery including smart plugs; stop minting devices per CT; entity links

**Wave:** 3 · **Depends on:** K01, S13 · **Size:** M · **Decisions:** DEC-04, 05, 06, 08 · **Interfaces:** §9 endpoints routes · **Analysis:** `analysis/A-…` C1, C4, C10, WP-A5

## Objective
Discovery proposes Measurement Endpoints: CT channels, plus single-load power entities as **candidate** single-subject endpoints. It no longer creates one device per CT. Devices come from HA devices that own load-evidence entities, from user declarations, or from promoted unknown profiles. MEASURED becomes possible only through a confirmed single-subject scope.

## Steps
1. Discovery:
   - CT monitor channels become endpoints (`kind=CT`);
   - a bare `sensor.*_power` whose HA device is an appliance or plug becomes an endpoint candidate with `kind=SMART_PLUG` (or `APPLIANCE_MONITOR`), `scope=SINGLE_SUBJECT`, `scope_status=CANDIDATE` and `scope_subject_id` = the HA-device-derived Physical Device;
   - ingestion of candidate endpoints stays off until accepted (DEC-06).
2. Stop minting `dev_{ct_id}` (`discovery.py:391`) and `dev_{base}` (`:367`). Legacy rows flagged `legacy_ct_device=1` by S13 are **not** deleted. They are offered for retirement in the panel. While their mapping edge still exists they keep producing estimates, so HA entities do not break (DEC-04).
3. `POST /endpoints/{id}/scope` with `{"action": "confirm"|"reject", "subject_id"?}` and `PUT /endpoints/{id}` (display name, enabled). Every change appends a config revision or event; nothing is overwritten.
4. Entity links: one-to-many is allowed (DEC-08). `ambiguous_entities()` stops refusing and returns diagnostics only.
5. The vocabulary rule: no retired "dedicated" naming (ADR-0034, `test_docs_vocabulary.py`).

## Tests
- A plug is discovered as a candidate endpoint, not as a CT channel or a device.
- Plug samples are ingested only after confirm.
- A confirmed plug yields MEASURED for its subject (with I5 or a fake).
- A circuit CT can never be auto single-subject.
- A legacy `dev_<ct>` device survives with its id and HA unique id unchanged.
- One HA entity linked to two components is accepted.
