# L2 — Fixed and Adjustable model classes on a delta basis

**Wave:** 3 · **Depends on:** L1 · **Size:** M · **Decisions:** DEC-33, 34 · **Design:** §9 · **Analysis:** `analysis/F-…` C1, C5, C8, WP-F2 · **Closes:** AT-03

## Objective
`FixedPowerModel` covers standby, idle, active and transient levels plus transition ΔP, using median/MAD. `AdjustableModel` is a monotone or piecewise P(x) over any declared control variable. Both are fitted on device-attributed ΔP. OFF never means 0 W.

## Owns
- new `engine/wattmosaic_engine/models/fixed.py`, `models/adjustable.py`
- `engine/wattmosaic_engine/models/base.py`: remove the `OFF_STATES_DEFAULT` idle/standby collapse (`:51-52`) and the `or 0.0` fallbacks
- `engine/wattmosaic_engine/models/device_profile.py`: declared priors map onto the same specs
- new `tests/unit/test_model_fixed.py`, `tests/unit/test_model_adjustable.py`

## Steps
1. Fixed:
   - level stats from ABSOLUTE_ANCHOR observations (single-subject endpoint) when available;
   - otherwise standby is derived from ΔP chains (OFF→STANDBY step) plus residual-baseline steps (T3 events);
   - variability is MAD-based (`base.py:77-120` helpers).
2. Adjustable:
   - unify dimmable, variable-speed and `AdjustableResponse` (`device_profile.py:48-195`);
   - the control variable is declared, with no hard-coded vocabulary and no `_MAX_RPM`;
   - PAVA when monotone is declared or validated;
   - report `x_coverage`.
3. Declared priors (DEC-33) seed the spec as a prior and are never overwritten. A discrepancy beyond 3σ is surfaced as a diagnostic item.
4. The class comes from the declared `LoadShape`, defaulting to FIXED. Learning may propose a change (L5 records the suggestion) and never switches silently (DEC-34). Delete the keyword-based `infer_model_type` from the v3 path.

## Tests
- **AT-03:** a 7 W standby on a shared circuit, learned from ΔP plus a residual-baseline step, gives STANDBY with mean ≈ 7 after an OFF transition (never 0). The TV on a plug gives 3 W from the anchor.
- A non-linear dimmer curve is recovered without assuming P=kx.
- An unknown level gives `None`.
- An inrush outlier does not move the MAD-based variability by more than 10 %.
