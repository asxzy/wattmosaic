# I2 — Temporal response matching and evidence A–H

**Wave:** 3 · **Depends on:** K04, K06, K08 · **Size:** M · **Design:** §7 (evidence) · **Analysis:** `analysis/D-…` §1 rows §7.3/§7.4, WP-D3 · **Closes:** AT-05

## Objective
Score each candidate against the **whole event window** with a temporal response kernel, and compute the eight HB §7.4 evidence terms. Correct for dependence between terms that derive from the same ΔY. The output feeds I3 as priors and penalties, never as a winner-take-all choice.

## Owns
- new `engine/wattmosaic_engine/inference/temporal.py`, `inference/evidence.py`
- new `engine/wattmosaic_engine/inference/evidence_weights.json` (versioned config)
- new `tests/unit/test_temporal.py`, `tests/unit/test_evidence.py`
- `tests/acceptance/test_at05_response_delays.py` (remove xfail)

## Steps
1. `expected_response(candidate, transition, attrs, tau_grid)`:
   - use `ModelSetView.expected_window` when a kernel exists (L3);
   - else a step at `response_delay_s` from the declared `DevicePowerProfile` (`models/device_profile.py:223-227`);
   - else an instantaneous step.
2. Term A is the Gaussian log-likelihood of the observed window samples vs the expected mean and σ, over the full window, with the onset uncertainty marginalised over a grid of ±`timestamp_uncertainty_s`.
3. Terms B–H per design §7. Each term is clipped to [−10, 2] nats. C, D and E are scaled by 1/√3 (dependence correction). The weights file carries a `version` that is recorded on each `CandidateHypothesis.evidence_terms["_weights_version"]`.
4. A sign disagreement is a hard rejection **only** for a confirmed consumer with an ON transition and a ΔY clearly negative beyond 3σ. Otherwise it is a penalty.

## Tests
- **AT-05:** X07 light (τ≈0), blind (transient then standby) and HVAC (compressor +90 s) are each scored highest at their true τ. The HVAC step is matched, not dropped as unanchored (the old `engine.py:1116-1124` behaviour).
- Terms C+D+E derived from one reader are not double-weighted: a test with duplicated readers shows the score unchanged within 1 %.
- The weights version is recorded.
- Every term stays within its clip range.
