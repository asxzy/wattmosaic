# I5 — Estimate assembly, status, confidence, per-estimate provenance

**Wave:** 4 · **Depends on:** I3, I4 · **Size:** M · **Decisions:** DEC-05, 19, 20, 24 · **Design:** §7 (status table, confidence) · **Analysis:** `analysis/D-…` C1–C7, C12, WP-D6 · **Closes:** AT-14

## Objective
Turn solutions and identifiability reports into `DeviceEstimate`s and `DeviceGroupEstimate`s per the HB §8.5 status table:
- group sums are published as groups, never dumped into residual;
- members are null;
- MEASURED comes only from single-subject endpoints;
- provenance is per estimate and lists only inputs that changed the estimate;
- confidence has 7 components.

## Owns
- new `engine/wattmosaic_engine/inference/estimates.py`, `inference/confidence_v3.py`
- new `tests/unit/test_estimates.py`
- `tests/acceptance/test_at14_single_count.py` (remove xfail)

## Steps
1. Apply design §7's status table exactly. `allocation_rule` stays None unless an explicit allocation feature exists (none in 3.0, DEC-20).
2. `ReconciliationV3`: Σ identified (each subject once), Σ group sums, Σ unknown, residual. All terms are nullable.
3. Confidence components:
   - measurement_quality from reader quality;
   - event_association from match history (I6, a fake before then);
   - model_fit from model residual;
   - topology_consistency from the weakest edge lifecycle on the path;
   - identifiability from the null-space share;
   - sample_coverage from the independent-event count;
   - freshness from age.

   Not blended. The docstring states "not a probability".
4. Provenance: `source_reader_ids` = rows with non-zero weight on the subject's column; `evidence_refs` = reader evidence and HA events in the group that entered the solve; versions = the operator's triple. **Do not** copy a batch-wide ledger (`engine.py:958-960`).
