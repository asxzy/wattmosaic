# I3 — Joint delta and absolute solver: robust, signed, covariance-aware, with unknown columns

**Wave:** 3 · **Depends on:** K08, X02 · **Size:** L · **Decisions:** DEC-21, 23 · **Design:** §7 (joint solve) · **Analysis:** `analysis/D-…` C8, WP-D4, traps 7–10 · **Closes:** AT-06, AT-08, AT-09

## Objective
Solve HB §8.1–8.2 jointly over the candidate set:
- a robust (Huber/IRLS) objective;
- signed bounds per transition and sign class;
- learned λ_u scope-located unknown columns;
- model and temporal penalties;
- covariance-aware or conservative weighting of nested readers;
- single-subject endpoint anchors.

The same objective in absolute mode serves `infer_steady`.

## Steps
1. `solve_joint(operator, delta_y, sigma, candidates, priors, unknown_priors, mode) -> JointSolution` (K08):
   - variables = candidate subjects + unknown columns;
   - bounds per design §7;
   - IRLS (≤ 20 iterations) around `solve_constrained_nnls` (`electrical/solver.py:378-575`), which already supports per-variable `lower`/`upper` and a prior plus ridge.
2. Posterior covariance `cov = (Aᵀ W A + Λ)^-1`, or None when singular. I4 consumes it.
3. Covariance per design §7: GLS when Σ is estimable (≥ 30 shared events; Σ is estimated in `covariance.py` from historical residuals), else σ̃ = σ·√k from `RowMeta.sigma_inflation`.
4. Anchors: rows of a single-subject endpoint get weight ×10 and σ = max(σ_r, 0.5 W).
5. Hold `test_safety_invariants.py:202-226` (no `max(0, <measurement>)`). Signed deltas keep their sign.

## Tests
- **AT-06:** HB §7.7 example. A +45, B +60, Main +110 with the lights and TV as candidates. The solution separates them, ≈5 W lands in ΔU on Main, and no greedy credit happens.
- **AT-08:** TV on a plug (3 W standby, then 88 W) on a noisy circuit (σ = 20 W). The TV estimate stays within plug σ, and circuit noise lands on other candidates or ΔU.
- **AT-09:** one load seen by 3 nested readers. The posterior variance is ≥ 0.9× the single-reader variance, and the load is counted once.
- An OFF transition gives a negative ΔP (not clipped). Battery discharge ΔP < 0 is preserved.
- Higher λ_u moves power from ΔU to candidates monotonically, and the diagnostic reports the share.
- IRLS converges on 1 000 random well-posed problems. A non-converged solve is flagged, not hidden.
